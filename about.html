♙ 01 — The Story Welcome to SRCA

A mother-daughter bond that became a chess movement

Founded by Women International Master (WIM) Shivika Rohilla, her mother Dr. Nidhi Bhasin Rohilla, and her first coach, National Instructor Yogender Sharma, SRCA is dedicated to promoting the art of chess and nurturing talent with passion and precision.

Shivika's chess journey is a story of dedication, resilience and familial support. Playing chess for over a decade, she has honed her skills through countless hours of practice and unwavering commitment. From the start, her mother has been her greatest supporter — travelling with her to tournaments, investing in her growth and sharing every victory and lesson. That bond inspired SRCA's vision: empowering young players to chase their dreams in chess.

At SRCA we believe chess is not just a game; it is a powerful tool for building critical thinking, strategic foresight and resilience. Our curriculum combines foundational skills, advanced tactics and mental-strength training for players at every level — from freshers to aspiring professionals.

Join us at SRCA as we redefine what's possible in chess, one move at a time.
