Checkmate and Chill: Delhi Parents Bonding With Kids Over Chess Therapy

Delhi’s smartest families are making their move.

22 Aug 2025 SRCA Team

Some families spend weekends on screens. Others? They're discovering chess as the ultimate family therapy — where kids learn life skills, parents reconnect, and coffee-fuelled laughter fills the room.

That's where SRCA and its initiative Chess Coffee Connect (CCC) come in.

  • SRCA → Delhi's fastest-growing offline chess academy, focused on children's personal growth both on and off the board.
  • Chess Coffee Connect → An SRCA initiative to make chess fun, casual, and social. It's Delhi NCR's biggest chess community, with events across CP, Gurgaon, Noida, and beyond. No age bar. Just vibes, coffee, and checkmates.

Chess as Family Therapy (Yes, That's a Thing!)

Chess isn't only about pawns and rooks — it's about patience, memory, decision-making, and bonding. A single 30-minute game can sharpen the mind and strengthen relationships.

At SRCA & CCC:

  • Kids discover chess as a tool for academic focus & personal growth.
  • Parents finally get a screen-free bonding activity that is equal parts fun and brainy.
  • Families join a community of 1,000+ chess lovers, where learning meets laughter.

Why Families Choose SRCA + CCC

  • SRCA Classes: Structured coaching for kids → skill growth, confidence, and tournament prep.
  • CCC Events: Coffee-house blitz nights, Sunday tournaments, and casual matches with no age bar.
  • Exclusive Offer for Girls: Get 10% OFF on all offline SRCA classes — because every queen deserves to rule the board.
  • Demo Classes for Freshers & Beginners: Perfect for kids starting their journey.
  • Girl Child Promotion: SRCA is committed to empowering more girls in chess, creating equal opportunities and dedicated mentorship.

Why We're Different

  • WFM Shivika Rohilla, SRCA's founder, isn't just a WFM — she's a movement. With her passion for chess, she's uplifting the entire chess culture in Delhi.
  • Dual Experience: Learn at SRCA, play at CCC. One builds skills, the other builds community.
  • Family-Friendly Edge: Parent–child blitz nights, Chess & Chai evenings, and casual CCC events across the city.

5 Funny Things That Happen When Parents Take Chess Too Seriously

  1. You "castle" your weekend plans.
  2. Every family debate turns into a "positional imbalance."
  3. You shout "fork!" — but it's at the dinner table.
  4. Your kid outplays you with a gambit, and you're both jealous & proud.
  5. You secretly Google "how to beat my 9-year-old at chess."
