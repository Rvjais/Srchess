Discover the Power of In-Person Chess Events: Practice Like a Pro

Why over-the-board tournaments matter — plus SRCA x Epsom Chess Club’s first Rapid tournament in the UK.

19 Jun 2025 SRCA Team

In the digital age, online chess is booming — but if you truly want to level up your game, nothing beats the power of offline chess tournaments. From rising prodigies like Gukesh D and R Praggnanandhaa to legends like Viswanathan Anand and Anish Giri, one common factor in their journeys stands out: consistent participation in serious, over-the-board chess events.

Whether you're a beginner, an ambitious club player, or a parent looking to nurture your child's potential, offline tournaments are where true growth begins.

And if you're in the UK chess community, there's no better time to dive in!

Why Offline Chess is a Game-Changer

1. Real-Time Pressure Builds Real Skill

Offline games demand focus, clock management, and decision-making under pressure. Gukesh and Praggnanandhaa have often credited their sharpness to playing hundreds of real-world tournaments, where every move counts.

2. Develops Tournament Temperament

Unlike casual online blitz, over-the-board play teaches discipline, etiquette, and emotional control. Anand's world championship prep involved intense practice matches in tournament conditions — something you can simulate only offline.

3. True Chess Connections

Offline events like the Chess Coffee Connect by SRCA not only offer you a chance to compete but also to meet like-minded players, potential coaches, and lifelong friends. Some of the strongest bonds in chess are forged across the board.
