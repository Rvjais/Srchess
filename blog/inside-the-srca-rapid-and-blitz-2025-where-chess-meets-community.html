Inside the SRCA Rapid & Blitz 2025: Where Chess Meets Community

A recap of the SRCA Rapid Play & Blitz Battle 2025 in Noida — the next big chapter of Chess Coffee Connect.

03 Oct 2025 SRCA Team

The SRCA Rapid Play & Blitz Battle 2025 wasn't just another tournament — it was the next big chapter of Chess Coffee Connect (CCC), Delhi NCR's fastest-growing chess community powered by SRCA.

What started as weekly café-style meetups where players of all ages sipped coffee, battled on the board, and made new chess friends has now evolved into flagship events that are putting Delhi NCR on the chess map.

From Coffee Tables to Tournament Halls

WFM Shivika Rohilla, the force behind SRCA and CCC, knows that chess isn't only about moves on the board — it's about building a tribe. Drawing from her own experience as a professional player, Shivika curated this first-of-its-kind event with Chess.com as prize sponsor and Binge Central, Candor Techspace, Noida as venue partner.

Just like CCC's weekly sessions, the vibe was warm, inclusive, and community-driven:

  • Air-conditioned comfort for parents
  • Ample parking (always a win in Noida!)
  • Café-style seating that kept things casual yet competitive

And of course, the CCC trademark fun elements carried over:

  • Lucky draws
  • Polaroid pics for Naanis, Daadis & the youngest players
  • Food tastings by BASIL
  • Lively interactions with Shivika herself, chatting with parents & players like family

Numbers That Prove the Hype

CCC has already been bringing together hundreds of players every month. The SRCA Rapid Play & Blitz Battle 2025 scaled that energy:

  • 67 players in the Age Category rounds
  • 47 in the Open section
  • 50 adrenaline junkies in the Blitz Battle

And yes — some CCC café prize winners redeemed their free tickets here, turning it into a reunion of Delhi NCR's most buzzing chess gang.

Parents + Players = Happy Community

Parents loved the on-time rounds, smooth coordination (thanks to Yogender Prakash), and engaging activities. Players loved the chance to test their skills while still feeling the CCC community energy — supportive, friendly, and unforgettable.

Giving Back Through Chess

Like CCC's ethos, the event wasn't just about competition. Partnering with Helping Hands NGO, SRCA directed part of the proceeds toward underprivileged kids learning chess for free at SRCA. This community-first approach is why CCC isn't just the biggest chess community in Delhi NCR, but also the most meaningful.

Next Stop: Halloween Chess

If the Rapid & Blitz Battle was a taste of the big leagues, get ready for the Halloween Chess Tournament on 26th October 2025 — another SRCA x CCC first-of-its-kind event. Expect costumes, spooky twists, and the same CCC vibe, just bigger and better.

Why Join Chess Coffee Connect?

Because it's not just about chess. CCC is where you:

  • Make friends across age groups (from school kids to working professionals to retirees)
  • Play different formats every week (blitz, rapid, bughouse, you name it!)
  • Earn prizes, passes, and now even entry into mega events like SRCA Rapid & Blitz
  • Sip coffee, chill, and still feel part of Delhi NCR's largest chess fam
