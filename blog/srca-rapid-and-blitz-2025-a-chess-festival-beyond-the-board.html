SRCA Rapid & Blitz 2025: A Chess Festival Beyond the Board

SRCA Rapid Play & Blitz Battle 2025 — 20th September, Candor Tech Park, Noida.

05 Sep 2025 SRCA Team

At SRCA & Chess Coffee Connect, we've always believed chess is more than just moves on a board. It's about families waiting together, kids learning patience, parents investing their time, and communities connecting through the love of the game.

Too often, we've seen players absorbed in tournaments while parents and partners wait endlessly on the sidelines. This time, we're changing that.

On 20th September 2025, we're bringing Delhi NCR a chess experience that goes beyond the board.

Two tournaments, one day

  • SRCA Rapid Play 2025 (15+10 | 5 Rounds)
  • SRCA Blitz Battle 2025 (3+2 | 7 Rounds)

Venue: Candor Tech Park, Sec 62, Noida

Prizes: Cash Prizes | Trophies | Exclusive Merch | Certificates

What's different?

Alongside the tournaments, we're curating interactive workshops and surprise activities designed for parents and partners — so the entire community shares the thrill, not just the players.

Guided by Arbiter Vivek Mitra, IM Vishal Sareen, IM Hemant Sharma, Yogender Prakash, and led by our founder WFM Shivika Rohilla, this promises to be one of NCR's most inclusive chess gatherings yet.

  • Final call — register before 18th Sept '25!
  • The players list will be shared in our WhatsApp community.
  • Chess-Results registration link coming soon.
