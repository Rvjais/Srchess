The Rise of Chess in Delhi NCR and Haryana

Tournaments, community formats and the organisers shaping one of India’s fastest-growing chess hubs.

18 Apr 2026 SRCA Team

Chess in Delhi NCR & Haryana is no longer confined to quiet tournament halls. It has transformed into a vibrant ecosystem of competition, community, and lifestyle.

With India producing global chess champions and increasing grassroots participation, the region is witnessing:

  • A surge in chess tournaments & weekend events
  • Rising interest from parents & young players
  • The emergence of community-based chess formats

From professional FIDE-rated tournaments to café-style chess gatherings, NCR is now one of India's fastest-growing chess hubs.

Top Chess Event Organisers in Delhi NCR & Haryana

1. Delhi Chess Association (DCA) – The Official Authority

The Delhi Chess Association (DCA) is the official governing body affiliated with AICF & FIDE.

Key highlights:

  • Organiser of the Delhi International Open (one of Asia's biggest events)
  • Conducts state championships & rating tournaments
  • Strong pathway for professional players

Best for: Serious competitive players aiming for ratings.

2. Skillcraft – Premium Competitive Chess

Founded by Sushant Tiwari, Skillcraft is known for high-stakes professional tournaments.

Key highlights:

  • Prize pools up to ₹15 lakh+
  • FIDE-rated classical tournaments
  • Premium venues like Thyagraj Stadium

Best for: Advanced & rating-focused players.

3. MAHARATHI Chess – Grassroots to Greatness

Founded by Gaurav & Vishnu, MAHARATHI is rapidly building future champions.

Key highlights:

  • Created 100+ FIDE-rated players
  • Strong grassroots focus
  • Innovative formats (puzzle rush, analysis sessions)

Best for: Aspiring players building their competitive journey.

4. Other Notable Organisers (NCR & Haryana)

  • Noida Chess Academy
  • Independent school & corporate organisers
  • District-level tournament hosts

Strength: Consistency & accessibility.

Limitation: Limited innovation & experience design.

Chess Coffee Connect (CCC): The Future of Chess

More Than Tournaments — A Complete Chess Experience

Chess Coffee Connect (CCC), powered by SR Chess Academy, is redefining how chess is experienced.

Founded by:

  • WIM Shivika Rohilla – Global chess player
  • Dr. Nidhi Bhasin Rohilla – Parent, mentor & visionary

Their combined player + parent perspective makes CCC uniquely powerful.

What Makes Chess Coffee Connect #1 Player & Parent Friendly?

1. First to Launch Location-Based Chess Communities

  • Hyperlocal events across NCR
  • Reduced travel time for families
  • More frequent participation

This solves one of the biggest pain points for parents.

2. Parents Are Part of the Experience

  • Networking zones
  • Café-style engagement
  • Interactive involvement

Not just kids — parents feel included & valued.

3. First to Introduce Sports Psychology in Events

  • Mindset workshops
  • Performance coaching
  • Emotional resilience sessions

Because chess is 80% mental strength.

4. Chess Merchandise & Cool Culture

  • Stylish chess gear
  • Unique chess cards for fun learning
  • Making chess cool & aspirational

5. Experience-Driven Events

  • Café tournaments
  • Community nights
  • Simuls & themed formats

Transforming chess into a lifestyle movement.
