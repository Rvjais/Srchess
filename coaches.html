♘ 02 — The Method How our faculty works

Individual attention, structured progress

Titled players teach the game; certified instructors build the foundations; psychologists and a nutritionist look after the player behind the pieces.

01

Small batches

Batches of just 6–12 players, so every coach knows every student's strengths, habits and the exact areas that need work.

02

Structured levels

Freshers → Intermediate → Advanced. NI Yogender Prakash leads the freshers' batch, IM Hemant Sharma the intermediate, and GM Tejas Bakre and IM Vishal Sareen the advanced groups.

03

Mind + game

Sports psychologists Dr. Nanaki Chadha and Aashritta Chaudhary, plus nutritionist Yumna Shamsi, support focus, emotional regulation and healthy tournament routines.
