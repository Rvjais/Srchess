IM Hemant Sharma

Intermediate Batch
International Master FIDE Trainer Intermediate Batch

Hemant Sharma is an International Master (IM) and FIDE Trainer (FT) based in New Delhi, India, with over two decades of chess experience since 1999. He has participated in prestigious international tournaments, including the Paris Open, Isle of Man Masters and the Zalakaros International Chess Championship, achieving his IM title in 2017.

As a coach since 2016, he has trained students worldwide, including notable players like IM Dushyant Sharma, GM Pranav Anand and FM Rosh Jain. Hemant also served as the coach for Team São Tomé & Príncipe at the 2022 Chess Olympiad and continues to guide students ranging from beginner to 2300 standard rating.

Highlights

  • Winner, India's Under-25 Championship (2010)
  • Best Rating Category Prize, Gibraltar Masters (2018)
  • Bronze medal, Dubai Junior Chess Championship (2005)
  • Played the Paris Open, Isle of Man Masters and Zalakaros International — IM title in 2017
  • Coach, Team São Tomé & Príncipe, 2022 Chess Olympiad
  • Students include IM Dushyant Sharma, GM Pranav Anand and FM Rosh Jain

GM Tejas Bakre

Advanced Batch
Grandmaster FIDE Senior Trainer Advanced Batch

Tejas Bakre became a Grandmaster in 2004, the first from Gujarat and 11th in India. He is also a FIDE Senior Trainer. He has represented India globally, earning awards from the Sports Ministry and setting a Guinness World Record in 2010.

As a coach, Tejas has trained numerous top players, including Grandmaster norm holders and International Masters. His student Hetul Shah set a Guinness World Record by defeating a Grandmaster at age 9. Tejas also coached Team India at the 2022 Chess Olympiad. Most recently his student IM Vantika Agrawal won a gold medal at the recently concluded Chess Olympiad.

Highlights

  • Grandmaster title in 2004 — first GM from Gujarat, 11th in India
  • Asian Sub-Junior Champion (1997)
  • Asian Junior Champion, twice
  • Three gold medals at the 1998 World Youth Olympics
  • Guinness World Record (2010) and Sports Ministry awards
  • Coach, Team India, 2022 Chess Olympiad; student IM Vantika Agrawal won Olympiad gold

Jaydeep Sharma

FIDE Instructor
FIDE Instructor Arena International Master Tournament organiser

With more than a decade of experience in chess coaching and player development, FIDE Instructor Jaydeep Sharma has established himself as one of Delhi's most respected chess mentors. A FIDE Certified Trainer since 2012 and Arena International Master (AIM), he has dedicated his career to nurturing players from beginners to competitive tournament performers.

Jaydeep is known for his structured, student-centric coaching methodology that emphasises strong fundamentals, tactical awareness, strategic planning, endgame mastery and practical tournament preparation. His personalised approach has helped numerous students build confidence, improve their ratings and achieve success at school, state, national and FIDE-rated events.

As the Founder of Eminent Chess Academy and an experienced International Tournament Organizer, Jaydeep has played a key role in promoting chess and creating competitive opportunities for aspiring players. His passion for the game, coupled with his commitment to excellence, makes him a trusted mentor for players of all ages and skill levels.

Train with FI Jaydeep Sharma and build the mindset, discipline and skills needed to excel on and off the chessboard.

  • More than a decade of coaching — beginners to competitive tournament players
