Beginners

Build on foundational skills with basic tactics and an introduction to strategic principles. Chess is not just a game of pieces, but of possibilities — every move you make reveals a new path.

Opening principles
  • Control of the centre
  • Developing pieces effectively
  • Early king safety and castling
Basic tactics & patterns
  • Recognising threats and simple captures
  • Pins, forks, skewers and discovered attacks
  • Introduction to simple combinations
  • Types of stalemate
Endgame basics
  • Checkmating patterns with major pieces (K+Q vs K, K+R vs K)
  • Basic pawn endings (K+P vs K)
Practical play
  • Guided games with feedback on mistakes
  • Simple exercises to reinforce concepts
Openings development
  • Fundamental repertoires for white and black (Italian Game, Sicilian basics)
  • Development and tempo in the opening
  • Capturing the hanging piece / pawn

Intermediate

Develop strategic thinking, positional understanding and advanced tactics. Strength in chess comes from perseverance; the greatest moves often arise from the toughest challenges.

Opening repertoire expansion
  • Deeper understanding of common openings (Ruy Lopez, Sicilian variations, Queen's Gambit)
  • Opening preparation and responding to the opponent's moves
Advanced tactics
  • Complex tactics and combinations
  • Removing the defender, deflection, decoy
  • Complex pins, skewers and forks
Positional concepts
  • Advanced pawn structures (isolated, backward, doubled)
  • Outposts and weak squares
  • Good vs. bad bishops, knights vs. bishops
Planning & strategy
  • Formulating a plan based on the position
  • Recognising and exploiting imbalances
  • Transitioning between opening, middlegame and endgame
Endgame strategy
  • Advanced rook endgames (e.g. the Vancura position)
  • Knight and bishop endgames
  • Opposition, triangulation and zugzwang
