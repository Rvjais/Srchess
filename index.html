Welcome to Shivika Rohilla Chess Academy!

WIM Shivika Rohilla

SRCA was founded with a vision to provide everything related to chess under one roof—from professional coaching and tournaments to corporate workshops and team-building events. Our goal is to nurture talent, inspire passion, and build a thriving chess community in Delhi. By combining expertise, innovation, and dedication, we aim to create a legacy of chess in Delhi, empowering players of all ages to achieve excellence on and off the board.

Join us to learn, grow, and become part of a movement that’s redefining chess culture in India!

The Journey to Mastery

Mastering chess is not an overnight achievement; it is a profound journey of intellectual growth. Each level represents a significant milestone in developing strategic foresight, emotional resilience, and the ability to think critically under pressure.

From understanding the fundamental movements of the pieces to orchestrating complex, multi-layered attacks, our structured pathway ensures that every student builds a solid foundation before advancing to high-level tournament play.

"Every chess master was once a beginner. The difference between a master and a beginner is that the master has failed more times than the beginner has even tried."
