Welcome to Shivika Rohilla Chess Academy

WIM Shivika Rohilla

SRCA was founded to bring everything chess under one roof — professional coaching, tournaments, corporate workshops and team-building events. Our goal is to nurture talent, inspire passion and build a thriving chess community in Delhi.

By combining expertise, innovation and dedication, we are creating a legacy of chess in Delhi — empowering players of all ages to achieve excellence on and off the board.

♖ 04 — The Ladder

The Journey to Mastery

Mastering chess is not an overnight achievement; it is a journey of intellectual growth. Each level is a milestone in developing strategic foresight, emotional resilience and the ability to think clearly under pressure.

From the fundamental movements of the pieces to orchestrating complex, multi-layered attacks, our structured pathway ensures every student builds a solid foundation before advancing to tournament play.

Every chess master was once a beginner. The difference is that the master has failed more times than the beginner has even tried.
