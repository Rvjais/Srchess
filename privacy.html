Privacy Policy for Shivika Rohilla Chess Academy

Shivika Rohilla Chess Academy (SRCA) values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. We respect and prioritise your privacy rights and aim to process your personal information transparently, securely, and in accordance with applicable laws.

This Privacy Policy outlines our policies and practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use our services (the “Service”) and provides information on your privacy rights and how the law protects you. By using our Service, you consent to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree, please stop using our website immediately. For inquiries, contact us at enquiries.srca@gmail.com.

Shivika Rohilla Chess Academy is committed to respecting your privacy and protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it may be used, who we may share it with, and how it is safeguarded.

Definitions and Interpretation

  • Account: Refers to an account required to access certain features of our website.
  • Cookie(s): A small text file placed on your device by our website to enhance your user experience.
  • Our website: This website, https://www.srchessacademy.com
  • SRCA / We / Us / Our: Refers to Shivika Rohilla Chess Academy.

What Data Do We Collect?

Depending on your use of our website, we may collect:

  • Personal identification information (e.g., name, email, contact details)
  • Demographic information (e.g., postal code)
  • Technical data (e.g., browser type, IP address, time zone settings)
  • Usage data (e.g., referring URLs, activities on our website)

How We Use Your Data

All data collected is stored securely and used to:

  • Manage accounts and access to the website
  • Tailor our website’s user experience
  • Supply information about SRCA programs and services
  • Analyse website usage to improve user experience

With your consent, we may use your data to inform you of our latest offers and updates.

Data Storage and Security

We keep your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined. Your data is secured using encryption and other protective measures to safeguard it from unauthorised access. Transmission over the internet is not fully secure, so we encourage caution when sharing personal data online.

Sharing Your Data

We may share your information with trusted third parties to facilitate our services. Any data shared will comply with this Privacy Policy and relevant legal standards.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve functionality, analytics, and personalised content delivery. You can manage cookie settings in your browser to control this activity.

Advertising Partners

Our website may contain advertisements from third-party ad servers or networks that use cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons. These technologies may automatically collect your IP address and other information to help measure ad effectiveness and personalise content. Note that Shivika Rohilla Chess Academy has no control over or access to these cookies. For more information on managing your privacy preferences regarding these cookies, please refer to your browser’s settings.

Third-Party Privacy Policies

This Privacy Policy does not extend to third-party websites or advertisers. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services linked on our website.

Analytics

We use third-party analytics tools, such as Google Analytics, to monitor and analyse website usage and improve our Service. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. For more details, please see Google’s Privacy Policy.

Advertising

We may collaborate with third-party services to display advertisements to support our Service. Google AdSense, for example, uses cookies to serve ads based on users’ visits to our website and other sites. You may opt out of DoubleClick cookie use for interest-based ads through Google’s Ads Settings.

Payments

For any paid products or services, we may use third-party payment processors. We do not store or collect payment details directly. Instead, payment information is handled by the third-party payment processors, governed by their respective Privacy Policies.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing, where applicable
