Shipping Policy – Shivika Rohilla Chess Academy

At Shivika Rohilla Chess Academy (SRCA), we ensure your chess sets, books, merchandise, and gear reach you safely and on time — whether you're in India or abroad.

Free Shipping in India

We offer free shipping on orders above ₹999 in India. Smaller orders carry a standard shipping charge of ₹50–₹100 (based on weight & location).

International Shipping

For deliveries outside India, please reach out via our chat option or email to know the actual product cost including international shipping.

Delivery Timelines

Within India:

  • Order Processing: 2–3 working days
  • Transit Time: 5–8 working days depending on your city or location

Outside India

  • Order Processing: 2–3 working days
  • Transit Time: 10–15 working days depending on the destination country

Delivery Partners

We ship via Blue Dart, DTDC, Delhivery, and other reliable services to ensure fast and safe delivery.

Tracking & Support

Once your order is shipped, we'll send you a tracking link via email or WhatsApp. Need help? Drop us a message via chat or write to enquiries.srca@gmail.com and our team will assist you promptly.
